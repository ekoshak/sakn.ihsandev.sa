AC care

Cleaning the AC filter regularly improves air quality, lowers your electricity bill, and extends the life of the unit. We recommend cleaning it monthly, or as needed.

How to clean the filter

  1. Switch off the AC and lift the front cover.
  2. Carefully slide out the two filter panels and remove the dust.
  3. Rinse them with water and let them dry completely.
  4. Slide them back in place and close the cover.

Outdoor unit on the balcony

Keep it free of obstructions and pigeon nests. If pigeons are nesting, let the building guard or supervisor know and they will help.

Annual deep clean
